The whole-corpus Obsoletes/Updates graph -- every RFC connected to another by one of those two relationship kinds, system-wide. Citations aren't part of this structural view (that graph explodes into the tens of thousands corpus-wide -- 513 for RFC 4035 alone); switch to Focus to pull in one RFC's full neighborhood, citation family included. Click "Load overview graph" below when you want it -- it isn't fetched or laid out automatically, since a cold build takes a couple of minutes and laying out ~9,930 nodes client-side takes real time too, not worth paying every time this tab is merely opened.

Starts from one RFC and expands outward hop by hop: hop 1 is its direct obsoletes/updates/ Citation Match/Topic Match connections, hop 2 adds their connections too, hop 3 one ring further. Citation Match (real citations, filtered to an exact keyword match) and Topic Match (whole-corpus BM25 text ranking, catches relatives Citation Match's exact match misses) are only computed for the RFC you focus on, not for its neighbors -- "shares this RFC's topic" isn't a well-defined idea two hops removed from it.
